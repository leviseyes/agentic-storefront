# Agentic Storefront

An open-source conversation-first shopping starter. Visitors can explore products, compare options, ask policy questions and explicitly change a cart through text, suggested replies or optional voice, without creating an account.

The core must work across product categories. The two fictional merchants are Fieldwork (outdoor goods) and Daybreak (coffee). Product facts, option names, use cases and policies belong in merchant configuration; category-specific assumptions do not belong in the engine. A curated catalog is intentional: up to 20 products, 100 variants each, 20 cart lines and 20 units per option.

The inherited warm paper, brown accent, self-hosted Manrope, editorial imagery and two-panel interaction remain the visual authority. This release expands that interface with collection rows, product options, comparison tables and a multi-item cart. On desktop the visual/cart stage sits left, conversation right; mobile stacks them. Cart content must never cover the conversation. Merchant imagery may change without changing interaction.

The examples are honestly labeled fictional. No bundled credentials, live merchant identity, private product CAD, customer data, or claims from another merchant. Default operation is a keyless scripted demo; AI/voice/Shopify require explicit server configuration. Optional photo tools and video are disabled until configured. Shopify remains responsible for checkout and orders.
